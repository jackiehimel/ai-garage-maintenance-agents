# Solvd maintenance offering: service competitors and agent libraries

Research date: 2026-10-26. Library, preview and registry unchanged. This extends the prior needs/alternatives study with the service-provider comparison it did not adequately cover.

## Direct answer and correction to the previous research scope

An alternatives researcher ran previously, but compared tools, ordinary assistants and a few service examples. That was not a sufficiently broad examination of Solvd-like engineering firms and maintenance-agent offerings. The short table's one or two citations per agent were selected examples, not the entire evidence register; however, some direct owner/workflow evidence remains limited. More references alone would not repair the missing service comparison.

This pass profiles **seven providers using 20 substantive first-party pages**: SoftServe (4), DataArt (3), EPAM (3), N-iX (3), PwC (3), Sphere (2), Capgemini (2). Solvd's Core AI page is additional company context. Related pages from one company are not independent corroboration. This is a purposive set of service-category comparators, not proof all seven compete for the same Solvd deals or budgets.

## What this changes about our recommendation

### 1. Treat the ten areas as checks inside a service, not proof of a complete maintenance operating model

The inspected flagship offers emphasize incident intake/triage, application context, investigation, engineering changes, testing, releases and closure. The current Garage proposal itemizes technical web/application checks. Those can be useful, but the menu does not by itself describe who handles a reported problem through resolution.

I would therefore qualify the earlier recommendation: **retaining ten coverage areas is defensible as a selective diagnostic menu; it is not yet the finished competitive service design.** The peer evidence does not justify deleting search/accessibility/performance/analytics, nor does their absence from some pages prove those firms cannot supply them. It does justify making operating responsibility more prominent than the count of check categories.

### 2. Give operational maintenance a clear center, with experience/measurement modules selected by application need

As a packaging recommendation, distinguish:

- **Operational checks and change evidence:** code/change review, dependency/security/lifecycle, identified integrations, availability/incidents and critical-task outcomes.
- **User-experience and measurement capabilities:** performance, accessibility, technical discovery and tracking/consent QA. These can be critical in the right application; they are not automatically the core reason every app owner buys maintenance.
- **Mobile release evidence:** select only for relevant distributed apps and release/platform obligations.

This is a proposed way to organize the offer, not a measured demand ranking or a new set of agent identities. Do not remove, rename or reorder the live library based on it without Jackie's decision.

### 3. Specify the work between a finding and a resolved problem

The service needs an explicit path for an incoming issue/alert, permitted context and evidence, a reviewed diagnosis/next action, owner/approval, the separately scoped repair/release executor, and evidence of completion. Existing incidents, tickets and runbooks are important inputs, not merely background to a metric report.

Our current monitoring/reporting boundary can remain. It means the provider must make the handoff and responsibility clear; it does not authorize us to copy competitors' autonomous code changes, production access, releases or data actions. A customer needing included rapid recovery is evaluating a materially different scope. No price equivalence or claim of a cheaper comparable outcome has been established.

### 4. Keep the newly found omissions visible without inventing more agents to match peers

The prior needs study identified recovery evidence and owner authority. The peer comparison adds commercial precedents for recovery planning, runbooks/knowledge, release coordination and corrective capacity. Put those in initial assessment and the operating contract; consider recurring modules only when actual evidence, triggers and decisions warrant them. Data/AI operations and FinOps appear in larger estates, but that does not establish a need to add them to every lightweight app.

These are service-design conclusions. Competitor marketing is evidence of an offer, not proof of buyer demand, actual ROI or the right Solvd price.

## Proof, prices and comparability

Most inspected pages are live service descriptions, capability catalogs or provider-authored cases with no reliable visible publication date. Their named sections and saved snapshots provide provenance; do not invent dates. A current offer page can establish what is marketed, while an empirical study answers a different question.

The named SoftServe Causeway case describes concrete operations, but headline outage/efficiency wording is not independently reproduced or attributed to the catalog's agents alone. N-iX's housing QA case is anonymous, large-scale and has inconsistent industry labeling; it is not a basis for target-market outcome claims. Other anonymized case summaries, logos, testimonials and headline percentage improvements are treated as provider assertions. None validates our agent accuracy or service outcomes.

The inspected pages generally do not publish comparable fixed fees. N-iX describes a flat monthly OpenText model and a fixed-cost calibration without dollar amounts. DataArt specifies billable enhancement capacity, not a universal included-fix allowance. Sphere's coverage windows are engagement-defined. Case-specific 24/7 delivery is not a guaranteed response SLA for every customer. No quote requests, purchases, interviews or service tests occurred.

Solvd's [Core AI page](https://solvd.com/services/core-ai/) supports comparing agent development, orchestration and human accountability as service capabilities. Its “agentic support” describes customer service; it does not establish a working maintenance library. No absence of other Solvd practices is inferred from one page.
